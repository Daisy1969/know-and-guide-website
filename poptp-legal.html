App licence and billing

When obtained through the App Store, PopTP is licensed under Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. Apple handles the upfront payment and provides instructions for requesting a refund.

Australian consumer rights

You retain any rights and remedies you have under the Australian Consumer Law. The Australian Competition and Consumer Commission explains consumer rights and guarantees.

Intended use

PopTP helps an adult record instructions already provided by a healthcare professional, set optional reminders and keep a personal record. For a child’s record, the parent or legal guardian sets up the plan and remains responsible for supervision.

Follow your prescriber’s instructions and check recognised label details before saving. Ask your doctor or pharmacist about doses, missed doses, interactions or changes to treatment. PopTP does not diagnose, prescribe, calculate doses, check interactions or provide emergency monitoring. For medical emergencies in Australia call Triple Zero (000).

Privacy and external services

The PopTP privacy policy describes local records, deletion and the optional services you can open. Third-party sites and services have their own terms and privacy practices.
