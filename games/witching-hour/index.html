A witch's guide to flying

01 / Find your rhythm. Tap Space, ↑, W, or the screen for a little lift. Keep tapping to stay up; pause your taps to drift down. Stay above the bottom storm clouds and fly through the gaps. Touching a storm cloud costs a heart.

02 / Watch the locals. Dodge ghosts and the yetis' flying boulders. Press X or tap the green spell button to clear nearby trouble. Your spell recharges in five seconds; storm clouds must be dodged.

03 / Beat all three levels. Each level lasts 600 m. Your broom steadily speeds up, cloud banks arrive closer together, and their openings narrow until the moon at 1,800 m. Starry violet clouds are safe and recharge spells; every eight charms restore a heart.
