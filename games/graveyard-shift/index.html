A survival guide for the night shift

01 / Tap, then let go. Press Space or touch the screen, then release within 0.48 seconds to jump. You have more time to release before it becomes a duck. Jump over headstones, gates, zombie hands, and staggering zombies. You can jump again once the stone wheel lands.

02 / Hold your nerve. Hold Space or the game screen for at least 0.48 seconds to duck. Keep holding until you’ve passed beneath a tree trunk; release to stand. A held press ducks without making you jump first.

03 / Outroll the night. Six 600 m levels lead to dawn: Cemetery Gates, Deadwood Crossing, The Haunted Chapel, Scarecrow Ridge, The Spider Cavern and Dawnwood Escape. Hills and troughs lead into a flat chapel floor. Leap its pews and coffins, then climb the ridge as scarecrows throw pumpkin heads that roll up and down the slope. Inside the cave, hold to duck under hanging spiders and ceiling spikes; jump egg clusters and floor spikes. Descend through Dawnwood, ducking bats and jumping thorny roots. The pace rises throughout the 3,600 m ride. Three hits end the ride. Press P or Escape to pause.
