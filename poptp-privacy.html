PopTP · iPhone & iPad

Privacy policy

Updated 19 November 2026 · Australia

PopTP, short for PopThatPill, is a medicine-record and reminder app for iPhone and iPad developed by Michael Harvey through Know & Guide. This policy covers the app, its optional services and the information you choose to send us.

Your health record stays on your device. PopTP has no developer-operated health-record server, app account, advertising or analytics SDK. Medicine names, conditions, labels and reports are not sent to an AI model. You decide whether to open an external service or share a summary.

1. Information held on your device

You can record medicine names, prescribed wording, doses, schedules, photos and label text; Taken/Skipped history; conditions, allergies and reactions; review answers; prescription-change history; recorded pharmacy quotes; a care role and an optional nickname. These records support your chosen schedule and summaries. They are user-entered or user-confirmed, not a clinical assessment.

Record files and retained migration backups use iOS file protection. The app’s health-data directory is excluded from device backups. PopTP has no Apple Health integration, cloud health-record sync or medication-data backup and import feature. Exported or shared copies are separate and follow the destination you choose.

Optional practice allows one fictional sample medicine. It starts empty and keeps sample entries in memory. Choosing Exit demo, backgrounding the app or restarting it discards the practice session. Practice does not request notification permission, schedule reminders or write to your saved plan.

2. Parent controls and a child's record

An adult manages one person's record: their own, or a child's as parent or legal guardian. The role confirmation is self-attestation, not verification of age, identity or guardianship. No birthdate or identity document is requested by PopTP.

The parent sets the prescribed plan and locks administrative controls before handing the device to the child. The child can view today's permitted plan and record actions. Parent codes and recovery keys, where configured, are protected through local Keychain verifiers and are not uploaded, included in reports or given to the app guide. Keep the code and recovery key private from the child and store the recovery key separately from the device. They are not an Apple Screen Time or device-wide lock.

3. Label reading, reminders and app guidance

Label recognition runs on the device. Camera access is requested when you choose to photograph a label; the system photo picker lets you select an image. Labels can contain personal details. Review the captured image, recognised wording and prescription schedule before saving. Original photos in your photo library are managed separately by iOS.

Reminders use local notifications with generic wording. PopTP asks for notification permission only after your action. iOS settings, Focus, sound and delivery behaviour can affect reminders. You can refuse permissions and continue using the local records.

The optional Apple on-device app guide receives only a fixed help topic, demo state and a general care-role choice. It selects authored app-help cards; it is not an open medical chatbot. It does not receive your medicines, conditions, labels, images, report text, parent code or recovery key. Built-in help remains available when the model is unavailable. No cloud or third-party AI fallback is implemented.

4. When you choose to use another service

  • Nearby pharmacies: after confirmation, Apple Maps receives the entered area, or your current area if you separately choose and allow location access. The search includes pharmacies and the area, not your medicine list or health conditions. Opening a result uses Apple Maps. No background location tracking is implemented.
  • PBS and TGA information: the public release configuration opens official reference websites after your action. General reference links do not include a saved medicine name, condition, patient detail or search query. Any product-specific external action must explain the identifier sent before opening. The private development build's older offline PBS catalogue is not included in the public release configuration.
  • Appointment summary: the preview and PDF are prepared locally. A summary leaves the app when you choose a share destination. Check the content and recipient first. The recipient and destination service control copies you share.
  • App Store purchase. One upfront App Store purchase includes all app features. Apple handles payment and your Apple Account information under its own policies. PopTP has no in-app purchase, payment form or developer-operated receipt service. The purchase does not send your medicine or health record to Apple.

These services have their own terms and privacy policies. See Apple privacy, PBS privacy and TGA privacy. External providers may process technical information outside Australia. This app does not promise that their processing is confined to Australia.

5. Retention, correction and deletion

Your local records remain until you change or erase them. Deleting a medicine stops its future schedule but retains past history and recorded quotes. In Privacy and safety, an authorised adult can choose Erase local app records to remove the app’s local records, saved images, retained history, care settings and scheduled app reminders. Parent access is required for a protected child record.

Erasure cannot remove PDFs, photos or other copies already shared or saved outside PopTP. Delete those through the receiving app or storage provider. Uninstalling is not a reliable way to clear Keychain items; use the in-app erasure controls first. PopTP cannot retrieve a lost local health record from a server.

You can correct records in the app. Because Know & Guide does not receive the local health record, we cannot remotely read, correct or erase it for you. For information you deliberately send to support, you can request access, correction or deletion using the contact below.

6. Support messages and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request, investigate the reported issue and handle privacy requests. We retain correspondence while needed to resolve the issue or meet applicable record-keeping obligations, then delete it when no longer needed. Email-provider records and backups have their own retention arrangements.

Do not send medicine labels, medical records, a child's identifying information, payment details, passwords, parent codes or recovery keys. Redact screenshots before sending them. No diagnostic report or attachment is automatically added by PopTP.

These static pages are hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub can process normal website requests and technical data, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. The pages add no advertising, analytics, forms, external fonts or tracking cookies. The site's optional browser tools read public website text only; they do not access the PopTP app or your health record.

7. Scope and policy changes

PopTP records confirmed instructions; it does not diagnose, prescribe, calculate a child's dose, validate medicine combinations or continuously monitor health. Interaction, allergy and medicine–condition safety checks are unavailable. Consult a doctor or pharmacist before making medical decisions or changing treatment. Support is not a clinical or emergency service.

Policy changes will appear here with a revised effective date. Any future account, health-data upload, analytics or connected clinical service needs new disclosures and any required consent before it is enabled.
