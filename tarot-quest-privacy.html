Tarot Quest Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026

Plain-language summary

Tarot Quest does not require an account, does not contain advertising, and does not use third-party analytics. Learning progress, settings, and journal entries are stored locally on your iPhone. Optional spoken-practice features use Apple-provided microphone and speech-recognition services.

1. Information stored on your device

Tarot Quest may store the following information locally on your iPhone:

  • learning progress, XP, levels, coins, streaks, and achievements;
  • voice preference and voice-speed settings;
  • saved tarot readings, interpretations, and journal entries; and
  • other app preferences needed to operate the learning experience.

This information is used only to provide the app’s features. Know & Guide does not operate a Tarot Quest user-account system or a remote journal database.

2. Microphone and speech recognition

Tarot Quest requests microphone and speech-recognition permission only when you choose spoken reading practice. Apple’s Speech framework may process speech on the device or through Apple services, depending on the device, language, and iOS configuration. Know & Guide does not receive or retain your voice recordings or speech transcripts through a separate server.

You can deny or withdraw these permissions at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security. The rest of the app remains available without spoken practice.

3. Information we do not collect through the app

The current version of Tarot Quest does not include:

  • third-party advertising;
  • third-party analytics or tracking SDKs;
  • location tracking;
  • contact-list access;
  • a user login or account profile; or
  • in-app purchases or payment-card collection.

Purchases and downloads are handled by Apple through the App Store under Apple’s own privacy and payment terms.

4. Support communications

If you contact support by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, device details, screenshots, and problem description. This information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the app. Do not send private journal entries, passwords, financial information, or other sensitive information.

5. Data deletion

You can delete individual journal entries inside the app where that option is available. Deleting Tarot Quest from your iPhone removes the app’s locally stored data unless it is preserved by an Apple device backup. Backup retention and deletion are controlled through your Apple and iCloud settings.

6. Children’s privacy

Tarot Quest is a general educational and entertainment app and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through a Know & Guide server.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Tarot Quest features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are published.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact winseral@knowandguide.com.
