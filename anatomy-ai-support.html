Report a technical or content problem

Please include the app version and build shown in Settings, your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, the body region and activity, and the steps that produced the problem. For narration or anatomy errors, include the structure, frame or question and a short description of the suspected error.

Screenshots are useful after removing personal details. Do not send client names, medical records, passwords, AI provider keys or backend access tokens. Avoid relying on disputed content until it has been checked with a qualified instructor.

No sound during a lesson

  1. Open Move or Massage, select a frame and press Play or Replay this explanation.
  2. Check that spoken explanations are enabled in Playback and voice settings and that an available English voice is selected.
  3. Check media volume and the current speaker, headphones or Bluetooth output. Lesson audio pauses when the app is interrupted, leaves the foreground or opens the teacher.
  4. Return to the lesson and press Play again. The app avoids unexpected automatic resumption after interruptions.

The lesson transcript remains available without sound. Narration uses the device's system voice and does not require a connected AI backend or microphone permission. Images are illustrated teaching sequences, not recorded practitioner videos.

Choosing a teacher

Offline study guide: uses bundled study material and works without a server; it is not a language model.

Apple on-device teacher: depends on supported Apple Intelligence hardware and an available system model. It helps choose the relevant authored study topic. If unavailable, select the offline guide.

Connected teacher: needs a compatible HTTPS backend, a separate app access token and your permission to send a question. Version 1.0 has no preconfigured hosted teacher address. Do not enter your provider API key into the app. Check the server operator's privacy details before approving it. A connection test checks access; it does not establish medical accuracy.

If a connected request fails, check the address, app access token and server availability, or use the offline guide. Do not send credentials to support. Starting a different backend connection requires renewed permission.

Progress, backups and privacy

Settings includes progress export and restore, progress reset, connected-teacher permission revocation and removal of the stored backend connection. Export important progress before resetting or replacing the app. Resetting does not delete previously exported files or device backups.

Use the remove-connection control before uninstalling to delete the saved access token. Removing a connection does not erase information already sent to a server. For privacy, access, correction or deletion requests relating to Know & Guide, email winseral@knowandguide.com. A separately operated backend must handle requests about data it holds.

Learning and safety

Anatomy AI supports study and fictional case reasoning. It does not diagnose, prescribe treatment, certify hands-on competence or clear a real client for massage. Practical techniques require qualified instruction and appropriate consent. The current generated artwork and markers remain subject to anatomical review. For a real medical concern, consult an appropriate health professional; for an emergency, use your local emergency service.
