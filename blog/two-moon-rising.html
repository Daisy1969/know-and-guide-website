Two Moon Rising — A Book Outline in Development

Posted: August 7, 2026   •   Category: Books & Ideas

Two Moon Rising is a new book project currently at the outline stage. Instead of keeping the idea behind closed doors until a full manuscript exists, I am sharing the working outline now so interested readers, collaborators and future-facing thinkers can see the project as it develops.

Open the Full Interactive Outline Return to Know & Guide

Why publish an outline?

Test the idea earlyShare the shape of the project before investing months in a full manuscript.
Invite useful feedbackGive thoughtful readers something concrete to react to rather than a one-line pitch.
Find the right collaboratorsMake it easier for people who connect with the concept to start a conversation.

The outline is being presented as an interactive web page so it can evolve. That format also makes it easy to send to people who may be interested in the concept without asking them to download a manuscript or presentation.

Current status: This is a working book outline, not a finished manuscript. The structure and presentation may continue to change as the project develops and feedback comes in.

What happens next

The next step is to put the outline in front of people who work with ambitious ideas and ask a simple question: is there something here worth taking further? Feedback at this stage can help decide what deserves deeper research, development and writing.

I am particularly interested in conversations that challenge the premise constructively, identify opportunities that have been missed, or point toward people and communities who may want to help develop the project.
