SoulPrint: A Moment to Reflect

15 November 2026 · Know & Guide

Sometimes a reading gives you words for something you’ve already felt. SoulPrint is a personal reading from HALO Vision and Cortnie Kairos. It draws on Human Design and astrology, using your birth details to sketch themes around how you listen to yourself, make decisions and bring ideas to life.

One idea worth coming back to: you don’t have to act the moment something feels exciting. Give it a little room. If it still feels right after the initial rush, you might find it easier to give it your full attention.

Two questions to come back to

  • What part of the reading reminds you of a moment you’ve actually lived?
  • What’s one decision you’d like to sleep on, and how does it feel when you return to it tomorrow?

Keep what feels useful and leave the rest. The reading is a prompt, not a rulebook.
