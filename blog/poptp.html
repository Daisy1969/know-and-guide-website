PopTP helps you keep a medicine record

19 November 2026 · Know & Guide

A medicine routine can involve labels, weekly schedules and questions for your next appointment. PopTP gives you a place on your iPhone or iPad to keep a record of prescribed instructions and what you’ve recorded each day.

Keep instructions and schedules together

Enter medicines manually or read a label on your device, then review the wording, dose and times before saving. In Today’s plan, record a scheduled entry as taken or skipped and undo an accidental entry. Local reminders are available when you allow notifications.

A parent or legal guardian can set up a child’s record and protect editing and settings with a separate code. The child can view today’s plan and record actions. A parent or guardian remains responsible for supervision.

Prepare for an appointment

Keep medicine history, conditions, allergies and notes together. Preview an appointment summary and decide whether to share a copy with your doctor or pharmacist. PopTP stores your record on the device and works without an app account.

Optional practice lets you add one fictional medicine and record it taken. The example is temporary and stays separate from your personal record.

Australian reference links

You can open official PBS and TGA information and choose to look for nearby pharmacies using Apple Maps. Any pharmacy quotes you record are your own dated entries. For advice on medicines, doses and interactions, speak with your pharmacist or prescriber.

App Store release

Version 1.0 is being prepared for App Store release in Australia. When available, one upfront App Store purchase will include all app features.
