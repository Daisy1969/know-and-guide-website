Smithers: a new look for the player

A hooded silhouette against moonlit ruins, five outfit colour stages and a showcase of movement and combat poses. The latest Smithers images bring the player design into focus.

October 23: the player takes shape

The new player design keeps the hood and face covering, with layered clothing and a clear, compact silhouette. In the supplied moonlit scene, dark teal sits against cold blue stone, broken timber and a bright full moon.

This gallery presents the three original images supplied for the October 23 visual update. The October 21 skills and chapters review remains below; these visuals have not been independently tested in the running game.

Five colours along the journey

The outfit showcase maps the visual progression from white at level 1 to blue at 6, slate at 11, crimson at 16 and dark teal at levels 22–25. The hood and covered face carry across all five, while belts, panels and equipment give each stage its own detail.

A look at movement and combat poses

The animation sheet is a visual development showcase covering idle, walk, run, jump and fall, sword attacks, slides and specials, and damage and death. Low running poses, sweeping sword arcs and sliding poses show how the character is being explored in motion.

October 21: skill choices, crafting and a bigger journey

Smithers has a 23-skill tree, a larger crafting catalogue and more developed later chapters. The choices extend from how she climbs and fights to the route through a castle, a mountain ascent and a crystal tower.

About the October 21 review: the systems and level layouts described below are based on an October 21, 2026 source review of the Unity project, not a fresh gameplay test. The four images in this earlier review are historical images from September 13 or 17, 2026 and show an earlier build. No new gameplay images were captured for that review.

Smithers is a side-scrolling fantasy action adventure being built in Unity. You guide a hooded fighter through towns, forests, marshes and strongholds. Sword fights and platforming carry the action; supplies, discoveries and Karma shape the decisions between them.

The biggest change from the September first look is progression. Character levels now provide points to allocate across a skill tree. Movement abilities, combat techniques and alchemy compete for those points, so preparing for the next chapter involves choosing how Smithers develops as well as what she carries.

23 skills, with choices behind the upgrades

The current tree contains 23 skills across neutral, white and black branches. Smithers earns one skill point per character level, with progression capped at level 25. Skills have rank requirements and prerequisites, and there is one free skill reset each chapter.

The tree covers climbing, Feather Fall, smoke mastery, dagger upgrades, sword techniques and alchemy. White and black allocations also affect Karma. A build therefore changes both Smithers' capabilities and her moral direction.

Level eligibility is only part of an unlock. Double Jump becomes eligible at character level 3 and Wind Rider at level 7. Both require the relevant skill allocation and prerequisites; reaching the level alone does not grant the ability.

Sword training also uses skill points. The former gold-purchase route is disabled. Gold remains part of shopping and preparation, while training draws on the same limited points as the rest of the tree.

Seven chapters, with more to navigate

Seven campaign scenes are enabled in the project. The early chapters establish the town, forest, jungle and marsh journey; the later layouts now give Smithers larger spaces and more specific objectives.

  1. Town, forest and cave. The opening chapter introduces the community and the search that takes Smithers away from home.
  2. The dark forest and jungle. The second chapter continues the trail through the forest and jungle setting.
  3. The Drowned Marsh. The third chapter carries the journey into dangerous wetlands and the mystery of the First Seal.
  4. The castle. Level 4 has seven main rooms and three secret chambers, giving the castle a structure to search rather than a single encounter space.
  5. The Broken Mountain. Level 5 is a continuous ascent with a sagging bridge and shelters along the climb.
  6. The Black Citadel. Level 6 centres on a five-floor crystal tower, with lava and a timed rescue lift.
  7. The final portal. Level 7 brings four ward crystals into the route to the final portal decision.

These are implemented layouts and systems identified in the project review. Their presence in the source does not establish how reliably every route, encounter or rescue plays through. That distinction matters as the chapters become more involved.

Crafting grows beyond the first five recipes

The current crafting catalogue contains 19 recipe entries across crafting levels 1–3. The September article described five recipes; that was the smaller catalogue at the time, not the limit of the current system. Alchemy is also represented in the skill tree, connecting preparation to character development.

Crafting gives gathered materials a purpose between encounters. Healing, ammunition and smoke supplies all feed back into the journey. A cave mushroom used for healing cannot also go into a smoke bomb; shared ingredients make preparation a choice about what the next stretch needs.

Karma reaches into the skill tree

Karma remains part of Smithers' identity. The skill system now makes white and black allocations another way to affect it, alongside the choices introduced in the original first look.

Historical reference: Karma and stealing in the September build

The September article recorded Karma from −100 to +100, starting at neutral. Taking a valuable cost 8 Karma. Positive Karma discounted shop prices, while sufficiently negative Karma could prevent trading. Those were the recorded values for that build, rather than freshly tested balance figures for this update.

The earlier lock-picking sequence asked the player to set pins and turn the lock before their timers expired. Entering a home and taking a valuable were separate decisions; the theft carried the Karma penalty.

A survivor looking for the truth

The story introduced in the September first look begins with Smithers losing her home and family in an attack led by an Ogre. She survives on the streets before a blind master takes her in and trains her. Her return sets up a search for the people and motives behind the attack.

That background gives the systems a common thread. Skills shape how she gets through an obstacle. Crafting turns exploration into supplies. Karma follows the choices she makes along the way. The current final chapter adds a portal decision at the end of that journey.

Where the work stands on October 21

The project has a broader skill system, more crafting entries and more developed later chapters. This update describes their state at the October 21 review; it does not date every change to a particular week.

Existing October 21 test reports record passing castle, citadel, tower, later-chapter and smoke suites. They also record two Level 2 failures and three Level 3 failures. Those reports were not rerun during this review, and no fresh gameplay test was completed for the October 21 review.

Smithers remains in development. The skill choices and chapter layouts give the adventure more depth, while the recorded failures leave concrete work to investigate before making stronger claims about the build. The next useful milestone is a tested journey through those systems together.
