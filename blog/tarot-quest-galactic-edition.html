New iPhone learning experience Tarot Quest:
Galactic Edition

Learn Tarot Through Play

Posted: July 18, 2026   •   Category: Learning Apps

Tarot Quest: Galactic Edition is a complete iPhone learning game designed to make tarot study memorable, visual, and enjoyable. Instead of relying on long lists of definitions, learners explore the full 78-card deck through short lessons, quizzes, daily practice, spoken guidance, interactive readings, and progress rewards.

Every card has been rebuilt in an original galactic fantasy theme, combining classic tarot symbolism with cosmic landscapes, detailed characters, purple-and-gold styling, and a consistent card frame.

What is included

All 78 cards22 Major Arcana and 56 Minor Arcana cards with original artwork.
Guided learningUpright and reversed meanings, keywords, symbols, and practical interpretations.
Games and quizzesGuess-the-card challenges, story exercises, XP, levels, coins, streaks, and achievements.
Reading practiceInteractive three-card readings that help learners connect cards into one clear story.
Male or female voiceChoose a preferred coaching voice, adjust its speed, and test it in Settings.
Private journalSave personal readings, interpretations, and learning progress locally on the iPhone.

Designed for independent learning

The app does not require an account or sign-in. Lessons, cards, quizzes, readings, settings, and the journal are available directly on the device. Optional microphone and speech-recognition features can be used for spoken reading practice, while typed practice remains available.

App Store status: Tarot Quest is being prepared for Apple App Review. The intended Australian launch price is A$3.33, or the closest price point made available by Apple.

Educational and entertainment purpose

Tarot Quest supports card study, reflective practice, and entertainment. It is not intended to provide medical, legal, financial, mental-health, or other professional advice.
