Introducing the New Yoga Veera Portal: A Digital Sanctuary for Inner Well-being

Published on April 14, 2026

I am incredibly excited to announce the launch of our brand-new Yoga Veera Portal—a dedicated, frictionless platform designed to bridge the gap between ancient yogic sciences and modern digital convenience.

Whether you are joining us in person or tuning in from across the globe, the Yoga Veera Portal is now your centralized hub for self-transformation.

Why We Built It

As our community of participants and expert facilitators continued to grow, we realized we needed a secure, beautiful, and intuitive space to manage our offerings. The goal was simple: remove the friction of scheduling and complicated check-ins, allowing you to focus entirely on your practice.

With over 10,000 active participants and 50+ guided sessions, the Yoga Veera Portal is engineered to scale with our global community.

What's Inside?

Our new platform offers exclusive access to powerful, life-altering practices directly curated for modern lifestyles:

Upa Yoga - Activate Your System

Access our guided sessions on Upa Yoga, a simple yet powerful set of 10 practices that activate your joints, muscles, and energy system. It is designed to bring immediate ease to your entire body.

Miracle of Mind - Own Your Day

In just 7 minutes, learn to explore the mechanics of the mind. Instead of acting as a source of stress, your mind can be transformed into a profound tool for your personal growth.

A Seamless Experience

We've completely overhauled the technical experience to make joining sessions effortless:

  • Instant Secure Access: Built on secure authentication, logging in to view your schedule and join sessions is as simple as a click.
  • Smart Online Check-in: For our digital sessions, we've introduced a seamless "Online Attendance" system. Facilitators can instantly open digital doors, allowing participants to securely check-in and jump straight into the Zoom room.
  • Physical Integration: Walking into an in-person session? Our facilitators are equipped with lightning-fast digital registers that sync your attendance instantly, completely replacing messy paper workflows.

Join Us

The science of inner well-being should be accessible to everyone, regardless of physical ability or prior experience.
