Tarot Quest Support

Tarot Quest: Galactic Edition is an iPhone learning game for studying all 78 tarot cards through lessons, quizzes, voice guidance, readings, and a private journal.

Contact support

Email: winseral@knowandguide.com

When contacting support, include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords, banking information, or private journal content.

Common questions

The app cannot hear my spoken reading

  1. Open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and allow Tarot Quest.
  2. Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Speech Recognition and allow Tarot Quest.
  3. Return to Tarot Quest, open Settings, and use the voice test.
  4. When using AirPods, confirm they are connected before opening spoken practice.

How do I change the coaching voice?

Open the gear icon in Tarot Quest Settings, choose Male or Female, adjust the voice speed, and tap the voice-test button.

Where are my journal entries and progress stored?

Your learning progress, preferences, and journal entries are stored locally on your iPhone. Tarot Quest does not require an account.

How do I reset permissions?

Open the iPhone Settings app, locate Tarot Quest, and review Microphone and Speech Recognition access. You can turn access off and on again, then restart Tarot Quest.

How do I reinstall the app?

Apps purchased through the App Store can normally be downloaded again from your Apple Account purchase history. Deleting Tarot Quest may also delete locally stored progress and journal entries, so keep any important personal notes elsewhere before removal.
