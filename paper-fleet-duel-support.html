FlickFleet Support

FlickFleet is the public App Store release of the project originally developed as Paper Fleet Duel. Use this page to download the game, report a problem, suggest an improvement, or find help.

Available on the Apple App Store

FlickFleet App Store version 1.0 has been approved for iOS and is publicly available.

Download FlickFleet

Report an issue

For bugs and reproducible game problems, open a GitHub issue. Please check the issue carefully before submitting because GitHub issue reports are public.

Open a GitHub Issue Email Support

When reporting a problem, include the game version, iPhone model, iOS version, game mode, AI difficulty, what happened, what you expected, and the steps needed to make it happen again. A screenshot is helpful when it does not contain private information.

Common questions

The other player or AI does not receive a turn

Wait for the attack line and ship movement animation to finish. If control does not pass to the next captain, record the game mode, difficulty, current level and the last action taken, then submit an issue using the button above.

My flick did not travel in the direction I expected

The shot direction is taken from the first instant of finger movement and is locked after launch. Begin with your finger on your own half, then make one clean movement toward the opposing fleet. Starting farther from the centre line produces more force.

Why did a ship move during an attack?

Ships may move inside their assigned waters while an attack is underway. Some ships remain anchored on a turn. Moving ships travel from their visible bow, and larger ships turn more slowly than small craft.

Why does the HUD sometimes hide or reduce itself?

The interface adapts to the current game state. Unavailable bonuses can be hidden, and HUD elements may minimise around ships to keep the play area clear.

Where are names, levels, credits and scores stored?

Player names, the AI name, campaign progress, credits, fleet purchases and high scores are stored locally on the iPhone. FlickFleet does not require a Know & Guide account.

Will deleting the game remove my progress?

Deleting the app normally removes its local data. Some information may remain in an Apple device or iCloud backup depending on your backup settings.

Release status

FlickFleet is now a released iPhone game rather than a private prototype. Development continues, so gameplay balance, fleet movement, AI difficulty, progression and interface details may still improve in future updates.
