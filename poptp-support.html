Help with your medicine record

Updated 18 November 2026 · iOS 27 / iPadOS 27 or later

PopTP helps an adult record prescribed instructions, view a schedule, record Taken or Skipped actions and prepare a summary. A parent or guardian can set up one child's record and lock administrative controls before handing over the device.

This page is published during release preparation. It does not announce public availability, clinical approval or a live medicine-interaction service.

Email PopTP support

This opens your email application. Include the app version, your device model, iOS/iPadOS version and steps that produced the problem. Do not send medical records, label photos, a child's identifying details, passwords, payment details, parent codes or recovery keys. Remove personal information from screenshots.

Support cannot remotely access your local record or change a prescription. It is not a clinical or emergency service. For an Australian medical emergency call 000; for medication questions contact your doctor or pharmacist.

Try and end the demo

Choose Try a demo to explore one clearly synthetic medicine. Demo changes stay in memory. Choose End demo at the top of a main screen to return to setup. Leaving or closing the app ends the demo. Your separate saved personal record is preserved; demo records are not a prescription to follow.

Add or correct a medicine

An authorised adult confirms who the record is for, then enters the medicine name, prescribed wording and times. You can read a label from a selected image or camera capture, but recognition can be wrong: review every field against the actual prescription before saving. Ask the prescriber or pharmacist about unclear or changed instructions. PopTP does not choose the dose or infer a replacement treatment.

Each local record is for one person. Do not mix a parent's and child's medicines. When-required or reference-only instructions need appropriate handling and do not create a repeating schedule. Taken, Skipped and Not recorded are distinct self-reported states; Undo corrects an accidental action.

Parent access and recovery

Set up a separate parent code and keep it private from the child. After entering the child's prescribed plan, choose Lock for child. Medicines, prices, official references, Health, sharing and administrative settings remain behind the parent lock. Parent access relocks when you hand over, leave or restart the app.

Where recovery is configured, save the recovery key separately from the child's device. Use the recovery action to set a new parent code with that key. An authenticated parent can manage recovery in the parent controls. A device passcode, Face ID, support email or a claim to be the parent does not replace the code or recovery key. Older records may need an unlocked parent to configure recovery first. If neither credential is available, contact support for guidance; we cannot retrieve a key or unlock a record remotely. Do not uninstall or erase records expecting support to restore them.

These are controls inside PopTP, not a device-wide Screen Time lock, verified guardianship or proof that a medicine was administered. Adult supervision remains necessary.

If a reminder does not appear

  1. Check that the medicine has a repeating schedule and reminders were enabled explicitly in PopTP.
  2. Check Settings → Notifications → PopTP, including notification permission, sound and alerts.
  3. Check Focus, silent settings, the device's date, time and timezone, and whether the current weekday is scheduled.
  4. Open Today to review the saved schedule. If PopTP reports that reminders could not be scheduled, resolve that message before relying on them.

Notifications are a convenience, not a guarantee of delivery or an instruction to take another dose. Do not take an extra or replacement dose because a notification was missed or repeated. Ask a pharmacist or prescriber what to do about a missed dose.

The guide or camera is unavailable

App guide always includes built-in help. Optional Apple on-device guidance needs supported Apple Intelligence hardware, settings, language and an available system model. If it is unavailable or refuses a request, use the authored help; no health record is sent to a fallback AI provider.

If camera access was refused, use the system photo picker or type the label wording. Review any recognition result before saving. You can adjust camera permission in iOS Settings. Simulator camera/model behaviour is not proof that the same feature has been checked on your device.

Official references, pharmacies and recorded prices

The public release opens official PBS or TGA reference pages after your action. Those pages are information sources, not a personalised interaction check or dose recommendation. The private development catalogue is a dated offline snapshot and is not bundled in the public release configuration.

Apple Maps pharmacy results do not establish stock, pharmacy registration or medicine prices. Your recorded quotes are user-entered, dated and unverified. Compare only matching products and collection terms. PopTP has no live pharmacy-price feed.

Purchases and restore

If a full-version unlock is offered, read the price shown by Apple's purchase sheet. Cancel to keep exploring the demo. If you already purchased, choose Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Pending approval is not a completed purchase. If the store is unavailable, try again when connected; do not repeatedly buy to resolve a restore problem. Refund requests and Apple Account/payment issues are handled through Apple.

A missing or revoked purchase does not hide an existing medicine plan, block Taken/Skip/Undo or export/erasure, or cancel its existing reminders. Paid creation and editing can require the full-version entitlement. Records carried over from the earlier private prototype retain their existing access; installing an upgrade does not ask you to purchase access to the plan you already recorded. Restore purchases may display Apple's Apple Account prompt.

No price or public purchase availability is promised by this support page. Store products must be available in the relevant storefront before they can be bought.

Summaries, deletion and privacy

Preview an appointment summary before choosing a share destination. Check the recipient and remove information you do not want to share. A report is a record of entered information and self-reported actions, not a clinician-approved assessment.

In Privacy and safety, an authorised adult can confirm erasure of local app records and reminders. This cannot be undone in PopTP. It does not remove copies already saved or shared elsewhere. The app has no server backup from which support can recover a lost record. Read the privacy policy before erasing or uninstalling.
