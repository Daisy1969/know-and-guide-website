Cylinders & Percentage Error

Learning Intent: Calculate Cylinder SA and justify measurement accuracy.

Cylinder Formula

A cylinder consists of two circles and one "rolled" rectangle.

$$TSA = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi rh$$

Where $2\pi rh$ is the Curved Surface Area.

Percentage Error

Used to see how close an estimate is to the real value.

$$\% Error = \left| \frac{Approx - Exact}{Exact} \right| \times 100$$

QUIZ Test Yourself

Q1. Cylinder Surface Area

Calculate the TSA of a cylinder with radius $3\text{cm}$ and height $10\text{cm}$ (to 1 d.p.).

Show Answer

$2\pi(3)^2 + 2\pi(3)(10) \approx 56.5 + 188.5 = 245.0\text{cm}^2$

Q2. Error Calculation

You estimated a length as $50\text{m}$, but the exact length is $48\text{m}$. Find the % Error.

Show Answer

$| (50 - 48) / 48 | \times 100 = (2 / 48) \times 100 \approx 4.17\%$
