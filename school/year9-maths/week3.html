Lesson 7

Composite 2D Shapes

Learning Intent: Calculate areas of simple and composite 2D shapes.

Key Strategy: Splitting & Subtracting

To find the area of a complex shape, split it into regions you know (Rectangles, Triangles, Circles).

Example: L-Shape

$$Area_{Total} = Area_{Rect1} + Area_{Rect2}$$

Practice Problem 7.1

A "House Profile" shape has a rectangular base ($12m \times 8m$) and a triangular roof with a vertical height of $5m$.

Calculate the total area:

Solution: $(12 \times 8) + (0.5 \times 12 \times 5) = 126m^2$

Lesson 8

Surface Area of Prisms

Learning Intent: Calculate SA using nets and formulas.

Rectangular Prism

Total area of all 6 rectangular faces.

$$TSA = 2(lw + lh + wh)$$

Triangular Prism

Total of 2 triangular ends + 3 rectangular sides.

$$TSA = (2 \times Area_{Tri}) + Area_{Rects}$$

Practice Problem 8.1

A rectangular shipping crate is $3m$ long, $2m$ wide, and $1.5m$ high. Calculate the total surface area to be painted.

Step 1: $lw = 3 \times 2 = 6$

Step 2: $lh = 3 \times 1.5 = 4.5$

Step 3: $wh = 2 \times 1.5 = 3$

Total: $2(6 + 4.5 + 3) = 27m^2$

Lesson 9

Cylinders & Percentage Error

Learning Intent: Calculate Cylinder SA and justify measurement accuracy.

Cylinder Formula

A cylinder consists of two circles and one "rolled" rectangle.

$$TSA = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi rh$$

Where $2\pi rh$ is the Curved Surface Area.

Percentage Error

Used to see how close an estimate is to the real value.

$$\% Error = \left| \frac{Approx - Exact}{Exact} \right| \times 100$$

Challenge Task

A soup can has a radius of $3.5cm$ and a height of $11cm$.

  1. Calculate the exact Total Surface Area using the $\pi$ button.
  2. Calculate the Area using $\pi \approx 3$.
  3. Determine the **Percentage Error** caused by using $\pi = 3$.
