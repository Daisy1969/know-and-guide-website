Area of Shapes

Learning Intent: Use best formulas to calculate area of composite shapes.

Area Formulas Cheat Sheet

Rectangle
Area = length × width
Circle
Area = πr2
Triangle
Area = ½ × base × height

Composite Shape Challenge

Strategy: Split the Shape

1. Rectangle Area: 10m × 6m = 60m2

2. Triangle Area: 0.5 × 10m × 4m = 20m2
