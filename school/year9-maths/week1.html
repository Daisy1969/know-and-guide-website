Terminating: The digits STOP.

Recurring: The digits repeat in a pattern forever.

Examples: 3.14159..., √2

Multiplication Law

When multiplying terms with the same base, ADD the indices.

am × an = am+n

Interactive Proof

y × y

Division Law

When dividing terms with the same base, SUBTRACT the indices.

am ÷ an = am-n
