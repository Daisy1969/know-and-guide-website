Lesson 8

Surface Area of Prisms

Learning Intent: Calculate SA using nets and formulas.

Rectangular Prism

Total area of all 6 rectangular faces.

$$TSA = 2(lw + lh + wh)$$

Triangular Prism

Total of 2 triangular ends + 3 rectangular sides.

$$TSA = (2 \times Area_{Tri}) + Area_{Rects}$$

QUIZ Test Yourself

  1. Calculate the TSA of a cube with side length $4\text{m}$.
  2. A box has dimensions $l=10, w=5, h=2$. Find its Total Surface Area.
Show Answers

1. $6 \times (4^2) = 6 \times 16 = 96\text{m}^2$

2. $2(50 + 20 + 10) = 2(80) = 160\text{units}^2$

Practice Problem 8.1

A rectangular shipping crate is $3\text{m}$ long, $2\text{m}$ wide, and $1.5\text{m}$ high. Calculate the total surface area to be painted.

Step 1: $lw = 3 \times 2 = 6$

Step 2: $lh = 3 \times 1.5 = 4.5$

Step 3: $wh = 2 \times 1.5 = 3$

Total: $2(6 + 4.5 + 3) = 27\text{m}^2$
